[Bloomreach](https://www.bloomreach.com) is a commerce experience platform combining AI-driven product search and discovery, marketing/personalization automation, and headless content management. It's built for B2B and B2C commerce companies, connecting marketing, merchandising, and content teams to deliver personalized customer experiences across channels like web, email, SMS, and push.

## What it does

Bloomreach's platform (the "Commerce Experience Cloud") is generally organized around a few core products:

- **Discovery**: AI-powered, semantic site search, product recommendations, and merchandising tools to improve on-site product findability and conversion.
- **Engagement**: marketing automation and personalization: uses customer data to send targeted messages across email, SMS, push, and web at the moments customers are most likely to convert.
- **Content (Experience Manager / brXM)**: a flexible, open-standards headless CMS (originally built on Hippo CMS, which Bloomreach acquired in 2016) for managing and delivering content, with APIs (REST, GraphQL) for integrating any frontend framework.
- **Loomi**: Bloomreach's AI layer, which connects first-party customer and product data with business metrics to make real-time personalization decisions across channels, including AI-driven email content and next-best-product recommendations.
- **Unified data platform**: combines customer, product, and behavioural data (including live catalog and inventory signals) as a shared foundation for the other products.

Out-of-the-box integrations are available with commerce platforms like Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, and Salesforce Commerce, and Bloomreach also offers a Commerce Accelerator and Commerce Connector SDK/GraphQL service for integrating with other commerce backends (e.g. commercetools, SAP Commerce Cloud).

## Key concepts

- **Discovery**: Bloomreach's AI search and merchandising product, focused on helping shoppers find relevant products.
- **Engagement**: Bloomreach's marketing automation and personalization product, orchestrating messages and offers across channels based on customer data.
- **Experience Manager (brXM)**: Bloomreach's headless content management system, exposing content via APIs (Delivery API, GraphQL) so any frontend technology can consume it.
- **Loomi**: Bloomreach's AI decisioning layer that connects data across products to personalize experiences in near real time.
- **Commerce Connector / Accelerator**: integration tooling (SDKs, GraphQL service, pre-built components) for connecting Bloomreach to a commerce backend with minimal custom development.
- **Unified customer/product data**: the shared data foundation (behavioural, transactional, catalog, inventory) that Discovery, Engagement, and Loomi all draw on to personalize experiences.
