[Salsify](https://www.salsify.com) is a Product Experience Management (PXM) platform. It centralizes product content in a system of record (PIM), lets brands enrich that content, and syndicates finished product experiences out to retailers, marketplaces, and owned ecommerce sites — helping brands keep product information consistent and accurate across every place shoppers encounter it (the "digital shelf").

## What it does

- **PIM (Product Information Management)**: centralizes all product content in one governed source of truth, organized around a common data model.
- **Syndication**: publishes product content out to retailer sites, marketplaces, and other consumer touch points from that central source.
- **Enhanced Content**: enriches product pages with below-the-fold content and rich media (image galleries, videos, comparison charts, PDFs).
- **Digital Shelf Analytics**: tracks and helps optimize how product content performs and appears across syndicated channels.
- **Supplier Experience Management (SXM)**: a separate but related offering for sourcing and onboarding supplier product content at scale.
- **Automation and AI**: workflow automation and AI-assisted tooling (including an AI assistant, "Angie") embedded across the platform.
- **Catalog Sites**: shareable, always up-to-date digital product catalogs.
- **GDSN Data Pool**: synchronizes standard supply chain, marketing, and ecommerce attributes globally via the GDSN network.

## Integration

Salsify is built API-first on a multi-tenant, cloud-hosted SaaS architecture, with a microservices backend designed to make integration into existing enterprise systems (ERP, MDM, PLM, other PIM tools) straightforward. Its developer hub provides guides for the REST API and webhooks, covering areas such as products, digital assets, imports, and export runs.

## Key concepts

- **Product Experience Management (PXM)**: the overall discipline/category Salsify operates in: creating and managing engaging, accurate shopping experiences across every channel a product appears in.
- **System of record**: the central, governed store of product content that all downstream channels pull from, intended to eliminate inconsistent or duplicated product data.
- **Digital shelf**: the collective set of places (retailer sites, marketplaces, search, owned sites) where shoppers encounter a brand's product content online.
- **Syndication network**: the set of retail and marketplace destinations a brand can publish product content to directly from Salsify.
- **Attributes and data model**: the structured schema (attributes, validation rules, data types) that defines how product information is organized; can be extended without rebuilding the whole schema.
- **Workflows**: configurable processes for managing tasks, approvals, and content readiness as product data moves toward publication.
- **Webhooks**: event-driven notifications that let external systems react to changes in Salsify data in real time.
